## Rules

1. A claim without a row here does not ship.
2. Comparisons against proprietary models are quoted, with a timestamp and a video link, never
   asserted in our own voice.
3. VRAM figures are labelled as our estimates, because the vendor publishes none.
4. Trademarked product names of other vendors are not used in comparative claims.
